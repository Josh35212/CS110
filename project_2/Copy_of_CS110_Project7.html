df_filtered = df_clean[df_clean['season'] != 'Unknown'].copy() # Remove rows with unknown season
df_filtered['decade'] = (df_filtered['year'] // 10) * 10 # Create decade column

decade_season_success = (
    df_filtered.groupby(['decade', 'season']) # Group by decade and season
    .agg(success_rate=('success1', 'mean')) # Calculate mean success rate
    .reset_index() # Reset index for plotting
)

plt.figure(figsize=(10,6))
sns.lineplot(data=decade_season_success, x='decade', y='success_rate', hue='season', marker='o') # Plot line chart
plt.title('Expedition Success Rate by Decade and Season')
plt.xlabel('Decade')
plt.ylabel('Success Rate')
plt.show()

My original question was: Is there a relationship between which season the expedition in the Himilayas takes place and the success rate of reaching the peak? This can be answered clearly by the visualizations created using this data set.

In the bar graph I compared the number of successes recorded during each of the four seasons. Since it was stored as a boolean, the bar graph was the best choice here to compare success vs failure. The conclusion that can be drawn here is that the season is a huge factor in influencing the success rate. spring and autumn have immensely more expeditions than summer and winter. Because of this we can assume that it is more risky to do expeditions in the summer and winter, and that assumption is correct as the failure rate is higher than the success rate in those two seasons. Additionally in the other two (spring and autumn) the success rate is higher than the failure rate.

In the line graph we can get a better understanding of these trends over time. Formatted over many decades, we can see that the winter and summer seasons have historically had a much more volatile success rate. spring and autumn have consistently remained about the same. In the end we can see that the seasons with the most consistent success rates are spring and autumn. therefore I can confidently say that as a result of this project, if I ever find myself planning an expidition in the Himilayas, I will not be going during the summer or winter.
